Installing Zebra

This guide will show you how to install Zebra on your device.

Zebra only works on iOS 9 and up.

Install process

  1. Open your package manager
  2. Go to "Sources"
  3. Tap "Edit"
  4. Tap "+". (differs on every package manager. could also say "add source".)
  5. Enter https://getzbra.com/repo/ in.
  6. Click "Add Source".
  7. Open the repo.
  8. Install the Zebra package.
  9. Respring.

You should now have Zebra on your device.
