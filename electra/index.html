Using Electra

Electra is a semi-untethered jailbreak for iOS 11.0-11.4.1. It is an app, so you'll have to sideload and resign every week.

Electra v1.0.2.

Downloads

  • The latest version of PlumeImpactor.
  • The latest version of Electra.
  • iTunes if you're on Windows.

Sideload process

  1. Plug your device in the computer.
  2. Open PlumeImpactor.
  3. Go to "Settings", then "Sign In".
  4. Enter your Apple ID credentials. If it asks for a verification code, enter it.
  5. Close the "Settings" and "Sign In" windows.
  6. Drag and drop the Electra iPA into PlumeImpactor.
  7. Click "Install".

The app should now be installed on the device. However, we still need to trust it for it to run.

Trusting the app

  1. Open Settings on the device.
  2. Go to General.
  3. Scroll down to Profiles and Device Management. Click on it.
  4. Click on your Apple ID.
  5. Click "Trust".

The app should now be able to run on your device.

Jailbreaking

  1. Open the Electra app.
  2. Click "Jailbreak".
  • If the device reboots or crashes unexpectedly not in a jailbroken state, re-run the jailbreak.

You should now be jailbroken with Electra. You should also have Sileo on your Home Screen.
