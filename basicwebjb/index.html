Using WebKitJailbreak

WebKitJailbreak is a semi-untethered jailbreak for iOS 0.0-0.0.0.

WebKitJailbreak on an iDevice.

Jailbreak process

  1. Open Safari
  2. Open the WebKitJailbreak website.
  3. Click on "Jailbreak".

WebKitJailbreak has now jailbroken your device, and you should have Package Manager on your home screen afterwards.

  • If the device reboots or crashes unexpectedly not in a jailbroken state, re-run the jailbreak.

To rejailbreak, follow this guide again.
