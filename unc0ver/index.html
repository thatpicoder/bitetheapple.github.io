Using Unc0ver

Jailbreak is a semi-untethered jailbreak for iOS 11.0-14.8. It is an app, so you'll have to sideload and resign every week.

If you are on iOS 12.0-12.0.1, stop and ping the "@Geniuses" role in the r/jailbreak Discord.

Make sure to delete any downloaded OTA updates, or else the jailbreak will fail.

Unc0ver supports iOS 14.6–14.8 only on A12–A13 devices (iPhone XS, XR, 11 Models, and iPhone SE (2nd generation).

Downloads

  • The latest version of Sideloadly.
  • Unc0ver (more details later)
  • iTunes if you're on Windows.

Unc0ver versions

  1. Unc0ver 5.3.1 for most devices.
  2. If you're on iOS 12.4.9-12.5.4, and 13.5.1-14.3, download Unc0ver 6.1.1 instead.
  3. If you're on iOS 12.5.5, download Unc0ver 7.0.2 isntead.
  4. If you're on iOS 14.6-14.8 on A12-A13 iPhones, get the latest version of Unc0ver.

Sideload process

  1. Plug your device in the computer.
  2. Open Sideloadly.
  3. Drag and drop the app into Sideloadly.
  4. Drag and drop the Unc0ver iPA into Sideloadly.
  5. Click "Start".
  6. Enter your Apple ID credentials. If it asks for a verification code, enter it.

The app should now be installed on the device. However, we still need to trust it for it to run.

Trusting the app

  1. Open Settings on the device.
  2. Go to General.
  3. Scroll down to Profiles and Device Management. Click on it.
  4. Click on your Apple ID.
  5. Click "Trust".

The app should now be able to run on your device.

Jailbreaking

  1. Reboot your phone (recommended).
  2. Open Unc0ver. (NOTE: If Unc0ver says the app has been tampered with, resideload.)
  3. Go to Unc0ver Settings, and enable the "Disable OTA updates" switch.
  4. Leave Settings.
  5. Click on "Jailbreak".
  6. Reboot your device when prompted.
  7. Open Unc0ver when your device reboots.
  8. Click "Jailbreak" again.
  • If the device reboots or crashes unexpectedly not in a jailbroken state, re-run the jailbreak.

You should now be jailbroken with Unc0ver. You should also have Cydia on your Home Screen.
