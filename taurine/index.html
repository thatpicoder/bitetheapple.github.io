Using jailbreak

Taurine is a semi-untethered jailbreak for iOS 14.0-14.8.1. It is an app, so you'll have to sideload and resign every week.

Taurine Promotion.

Downloads

  • The latest version of PlumeImpactor.
  • The latest version of Jailbreak.
  • iTunes if you're on Windows.

Sideload process

  1. Plug your device in the computer.
  2. Open PlumeImpactor.
  3. Go to "Settings", then "Sign In".
  4. Enter your Apple ID credentials. If it asks for a verification code, enter it.
  5. Close the "Settings" and "Sign In" windows.
  6. Drag and drop the Taurine iPA into PlumeImpactor.
  7. Click "Install".

The app should now be installed on the device. However, we still need to trust it for it to run.

Trusting the app

  1. Open Settings on the device.
  2. Go to General.
  3. Scroll down to Profiles and Device Management. Click on it.
  4. Click on your Apple ID.
  5. Click "Trust".

The app should now be able to run on your device.

Jailbreaking

  1. Open Taurine.
  2. Click "Jailbreak".
  • If the device reboots or crashes unexpectedly not in a jailbroken state, re-run the jailbreak.

You should now be jailbroken with Taurine. You should also have Sileo on your Home Screen.

Post-jailbreak process (one time)

  1. Open Sileo
  2. Tap on the "Search" tab
  3. Search for "libiosexec1"
  4. Tap "Modify", then Tap "Upgrade"
  5. Tap the "Queued" bar at the bottom of the page
  6. Tap "Confirm"
  7. Once finished, tap "Done"
  8. Search for "libhooker (common)", "PreferenceLoader", and "RocketBootstrap" and add them to the queue by pressing "Install"
  9. Tap the "Queued" bar at the bottom of the page
  10. Tap "Confirm"
  11. Once finished, tap 'Restart SpringBoard'
