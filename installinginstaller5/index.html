Installing Installer 5

This guide will show you how to install Installer on your device.

Installer 5 only works on iOS 9 and up.

Install process

  1. Open your package manager
  2. Go to "Sources"
  3. Tap "Edit"
  4. Tap "+". (differs on every package manager. could also say "add source".)
  5. Enter https://apptapp.me/repo/ in.
  6. Click "Add Source".
  7. Open the repo.
  8. Install the Installer 5 package.
  9. Respring.

You should now have Installer 5 on your device.
