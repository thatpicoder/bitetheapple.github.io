Using TotallyNotSpyware

TotallyNotSpyyware is a semi-untethered jailbreak for iOS 10.0-10.3.4.

TNS on an iPod Touch 6.

Jailbreak process

  1. Open Safari
  2. Open the TotallyNotSpyware website.
  3. Slide on the "Slide for spyware" slider.
  4. When you see the "Spyware announcement" prompt, press on "noot noot".

TNS has now jailbroken your device, and you should have Zebra on your home screen afterwards.

  • If the device reboots or crashes unexpectedly not in a jailbroken state, re-run the jailbreak.

To rejailbreak, follow this guide again.
