JailbreakMe Saffron

JailbreakMe Saffron is an untethered jailbreak for all iOS devices on iOS 4.3.3, however not on the iPad 2 on iOS versions 4.3-4.3.2, and can jailbreak the CDMA iPhone 4 from iOS versions 4.2.6-4.2.8.

  • If you're using an iPad 2 on iOS 4.3-4.3.2, you should follow the Aquila guide instead.

Jailbreaking

  1. Open Safari on your device.
  2. Go to jailbreakme.com/saffron/_/.
  3. Choose the latest PDF file that corresponds with your device.
  4. For example, an iPad 2 on iOS 4.3.3 is "iPad_4.3.3_8J2-3.pdf".

Cydia should appear on your Home screen.
