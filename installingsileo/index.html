Installing Sileo

This guide will show you how to install Sileo on your device.

Sileo only works on iOS 12 and up.

Install process

  1. Open your package manager
  2. Go to "Sources"
  3. Tap "Edit"
  4. Tap "+". (differs on every package manager. could also say "add source".)
  5. Enter repo.getsileo.app in.
  6. Click "Add Source".
  7. Open the repo.
  8. Install the Sileo package.
  9. Respring.

You should now have Sileo on your device.
