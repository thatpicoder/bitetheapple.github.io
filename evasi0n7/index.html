This guide will help you use the Evasi0n7 jailbreak.

Using Evasi0n7

Downloads

  • The latest version of Patched Evasi0n7
  • If you are using an iPhone 5S or 5C on iOS 7.0 (Build 11A466) use this version of Patched Evasi0n7 instead.

Jailbreaking

  1. Open the file called "patched-with-ipg-link-evasi0n7.exe". If you're on iPhone 5S/5C with iOS 7.0 (Build 11A466), it'll be called "ONLY_5c_7.0_evasi0n7.exe"
  2. Plug your device into your computer and trust the computer on device when prompted
  3. In Evasi0n7, press "Jailbreak". It'll take a while to download required files.
  4. Once your phone has rebooted, open the Evasi0n7 app on your home screen.
  5. When the phone reboots again, unlock the device. Evasion7 will reboot your device one last time. You should see the Evasi0n7 logo on boot.
  • Cydia should now be on your Home screen.
